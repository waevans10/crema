# Crema 1.3.0 — Grind & dose memory

Crema 1.3.0 remembers what you actually ground and dosed for every shot, and
shows your best shot on the current bean as a recipe to pull again. It also
stops a failing review from being re-billed every 15 minutes.

## Highlights

- **Grind and dose on every shot.** Two boxes sit next to the cup rating. They
  are prefilled from your last shot on the same bean (or the bean's target
  dose), so an unchanged recipe is just a tap on Save. Grind is free text, so
  numbers, clicks or "1 turn + 4" all work; dose accepts 5–30 g.
- **"Your best on <bean>" card.** The top of Now shows your highest-rated shot
  (4 or 5 stars) on the active bean as `Grind 12 · 18 g in → 36 g out`, with
  one tap to save it as the bean's recipe. Saving keeps the bean's profile and
  cup style.
- **Better-grounded reviews.** Claude now sees the grind and dose you recorded
  on the shots in its review window. Like tasting notes, values you add after a
  review has run are picked up by the next one.
- **No paid retry loop.** If a review fails, Crema waits 3 hours before trying
  that shot again instead of calling Claude on every timer tick. A new shot is
  still reviewed straight away, and the web "Ask AI" button or
  `crema review --force` retries immediately.
- **Stuck pushes can be cleared.** A profile push whose outcome is unknown can
  now be discarded from the web page or with `crema discard EDIT_ID`, after you
  have checked the machine.
- Community imports treat participant IDs case-insensitively, and
  `crema discard` prints a clear message instead of a traceback.

Grind and dose stay on your device: they are not added to the community share
bundle.

## Upgrading

Back up the SQLite database, update to `v1.3.0`, run `uv sync --frozen`, and
restart your Crema service. Python 3.13+ is still required. The two new shot
columns are added automatically on first start; older shots simply have no
grind or dose recorded.
